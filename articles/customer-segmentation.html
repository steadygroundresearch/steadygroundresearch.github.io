Customer Segmentation: Is It a Necessary Business Decision?

SG Research · 26 October 2026 · Data-Driven Decision Making

Customer segmentation means dividing customers into groups that share common characteristics, such as age, income, location or purchasing behaviour, so a business can serve each group more effectively. It is widely promoted as a smart move for any business that wants to grow, yet simply grouping customers guarantees neither useful insights nor better decisions.

So is it necessary? Not always. It is most likely to be worth the effort when customers differ in ways that matter to the business, the data needed to see those differences is available, the business can treat the groups differently, and it can tell whether doing so made a difference.

Three questions to ask before you segment

  1. What decision would we make better by understanding how our customers differ?
  2. Do our customers differ in ways that matter, and do we have enough data, which we are allowed to use, to see it?
  3. Can we act differently for different groups, and would the likely benefits justify the time and cost?

If any answer is no, segmentation may not be necessary or worthwhile. If it still looks worthwhile, check that the segments are measurable, substantial, accessible, differentiable and actionable.
