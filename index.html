Applied research that earns its place in the decision.

SG Research helps organisations unlock the value of their existing data by applying rigorous analytical methods to answer important strategic questions, identify opportunities, manage risks, and support better decisions.

Mission

What we exist to do

Most organisations collect far more data than they have the capacity to fully explore. As a result, valuable patterns, relationships, and insights often remain hidden due to limitations in time, tools, or specialised expertise. SG Research addresses that challenge by applying rigorous analytical methods to existing data, transforming data into practical, evidence-based insights that inform better decisions, improve performance, and support strategic objectives.

The analytical approach is built on techniques refined over an 18-year career in central banking, where they were applied to assess financial sector stability, and support evidence-based policymaking. Today, those same principles are applied more broadly to help organisations uncover meaningful insights from data collected over time, whether relating to customers, products, operations, or other areas where data is collected repeatedly over time.

Value Proposition

The question we answer

"What is our data telling us - and how can we use those insights to make better decisions?"

SG Research provides organisations with specialist analytical expertise without the need to build an in-house research function. The focus is on delivering decision-ready evidence that helps answer strategic questions-not simply producing dashboards or descriptive reports.
